=== fields ===
face: face shape, skin tone, notable features and makeup, with realistic proportions
eyes: eye color and shape, eyebrows, lashes
hair: color, length, style and texture
body: build, height impression and posture
outfit: every clothing item with its color and material, top to bottom
accessories?: jewelry, glasses, props held or worn
traits?: non-human traits made real (ears, horns, tail, wings): where they attach and their texture
expression: facial expression and what the eyes and mouth do
pose: body pose, hands and head angle
setting: the background as a real place
lighting: light source, direction and mood
camera: framing, lens and angle (for example medium shot, 50mm, eye level)

Keep each field to one sentence.

Give each field one or two precise sentences with colors and materials.

Write each field as plain descriptive prose.
=== style.tags ===
Write each field as short comma-separated tags.
=== style.mixed ===
